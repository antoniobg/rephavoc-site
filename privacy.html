RepHavoc Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

For questions about this policy, contact rephavoc@granell.dev.

Fitness data

Your exercise goals, logged reps, history, and reminder preferences are stored on your device. RepHavoc does not send this fitness data to our servers or RevenueCat. No account is required. The app does not currently access HealthKit. Device backups may include app data according to your device settings.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat helps us validate purchases, determine whether your Pro subscription is active, and understand subscription activity through its dashboard analytics. This involves an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and subscription information, and technical information such as app and operating-system versions. RevenueCat may process network information, including your IP address, when the app communicates with its service. We do not receive your payment-card details.

See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Reminders and tracking

Reminders are scheduled locally with your permission. You can disable them in the app or in iOS Settings. RepHavoc does not include advertising or use your data for cross-app advertising tracking.

Retention and choices

Archiving a goal retains its history. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to any device backups. Purchase records may remain with Apple and RevenueCat for subscription operation and applicable legal obligations. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

Contact rephavoc@granell.dev to ask about access, correction, or deletion of personal data processed through our subscription service. We may need information to identify the relevant purchase record. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights, including the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the site. We do not add analytics or advertising trackers to these pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
