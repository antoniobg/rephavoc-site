Need help? Email crystalabyss@granell.dev.

Include your iOS version, RepHavoc version, and what happened. Do not send passwords or payment-card information.

Goals

Three active goals are free. Pro unlocks more. Archive a goal to free a slot while keeping its history.

Reminders

Enable notifications in iOS Settings. Open RepHavoc regularly to replenish scheduled reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device and can be affected by Focus and notification settings.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases on the Pro screen if your subscription is missing. Use Manage Subscription or iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions to cancel. Removing the app does not cancel your subscription. TestFlight purchases run in Apple's test environment.

Data

Goals and rep history are local to your installation. Cloud sync is not currently available. Archiving keeps history; uninstalling may remove it.
